What this is

This is for a company that wants one plain practice in the middle of ordinary work. It is not a wellness challenge, and it is not a productivity hack. Pay, deadlines, and rest stay exactly as real as they were this morning.

Ganesh Subramanian comes to the team, or meets them on a call. He says, in short sentences, the same thing his free site Two Truths says: keep the measured world, and give a real time to the question of who is living it. Then the room is quiet.

What a session is

  • You write first. Say the size of the group, the place, and whether you want Cincinnati, your office, or a call.
  • There is no price on this page. The number is in the reply, before anything starts.
  • The session is a short talk and a sit. People may ask what they noticed. They are not assigned a score.
  • Ganesh does not report who was calm, who spoke, or who should be considered further along. There is no further along.

What it will not do

It will not track streaks, attendance as a contest, or a ladder of sessions. A person who misses the next one has missed a meeting. That is all.

Not therapy. Not medical advice. If sitting brings up something distressing, that person stops. A counselor or a doctor is the right next person. This service does not replace either.
