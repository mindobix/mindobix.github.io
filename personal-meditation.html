What this is

This is one person with Ganesh Subramanian. Bring ordinary life with you: family, work, money, the body, rest. None of that is in the way. The practice is not an exit from those things. It is a way of entering them with a looser grip.

The same words are free, any hour, at twotruths.net. This page is for when you want a person in the room, or on a call, the first few times — someone who will sit the time with you and will not turn it into a program.

What a session is

  • You write. Say a little about why now, and whether you want Cincinnati or a call.
  • There is no price list. The number for a single sit is in the reply, before you book.
  • The first meeting is a conversation and a sit. If it is not a fit, you both say so and stop.
  • You pick a length for the days between: ten minutes is enough. Twenty is enough. Longer is not better.
  • Ganesh does not keep a chart of your days. Missing one costs nothing he is counting.

What it will not do

It will not train you into a better watcher. The awareness is already running. What changes, when it changes, is that you stop taking the thinking to be the one who is aware. That is a return, not a skill you stack.

Not therapy. Not medical advice. If sitting brings up something distressing, stop. A counselor or a doctor is the right next person. Ganesh is not either of those.
