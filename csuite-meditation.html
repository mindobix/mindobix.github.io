What this is

This is the same practice as the enterprise service, held for the person who has to decide. It is not leadership coaching, and it is not a ladder of executive presence. Family, the body, money, and rest come in with you. They are not a distraction from the serious work. They are part of the life you are already in.

Ganesh Subramanian does not publish the names of the people he sits with. Write from the address you actually use. Say whether this is you alone or a small circle, and whether you want Cincinnati, your office, or a call.

What a session is

  • One conversation first, so both of you can tell if it fits.
  • No price on this page. The number is in the reply, before anything starts.
  • Then a sit. Plain words before it. Quiet during it. You may talk after, about what you noticed, not about how well you did.
  • You choose a length you can keep on the days Ganesh is not there. Ten minutes is a real sit.

What it will not do

It will not make you calmer so the quarter goes better. It may, and it may not. The ask is smaller and harder: stop treating the one who is aware as one more thing to improve. Nothing here is acquired. You return to what is already running.

Not therapy. Not medical advice. If sitting brings up something distressing, stop. A counselor or a doctor is the right next person. Ganesh is not a clinician.
