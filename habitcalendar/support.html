Support

Everything you need to build a habit and keep it. HabitCalendar is free, requires no account, and keeps your data on your own devices. If something here doesn't answer your question, write to us — a real person reads every message.

How do I add a habit?

Open the Habits tab and tap + New. Give your habit a name — meditation, exercise, reading, water, anything — and it's ready to track right away.

How do I mark a day done?

On the Calendar tab, just tap a day to mark it done. Tap it again to clear it. Use the Today button to jump back to the current day at any time.

Can I track more than one habit?

Yes — track as many habits as you like. Switch between them in a single tap, and see each one's own calendar and insights.

What do the Insights show?

The Insights tab shows your current streak, best streak, completion rate, recent daily progress, and a consistency map of the last several weeks — so you can see your momentum at a glance.

Do I need an account?

No. There's nothing to sign up for and no login. Open the app, add a habit, and start tapping.

Where is my data stored, and does it sync?

Your habits and history stay on your devices and sync privately between your iPhone and iPad through your own iCloud. There's no Mindobix server collecting your data, and nothing is sold or shared. See our Privacy Policy for details.

What happens if I miss a day?

Nothing is lost. Streaks are there to encourage you, not to punish you — miss a day and simply pick it back up. The calendar is a record, not a scoreboard.

How much does it cost?

HabitCalendar is free. There are no subscriptions, no ads, and no upsell.
