Support

Everything you need to sit. Awakening is free, requires no account, and keeps your practice on your own devices. If something here doesn't answer your question, write to us — a real person reads every message.

How do I start a sit?

Open Awakening and tap BEGIN to start sitting. Sit for as long as you like, then tap END to finish. You can also set a gentle timer beforehand if you prefer a fixed length.

How does it work on Apple TV?

Use the Siri Remote: click to select, and swipe to move between tabs. The daily reflection and your Awake log are fully navigable on the big screen, with the same calm experience as iPhone and iPad.

Do I need an account?

No. There's nothing to sign up for and no login. Open the app and begin. Your practice is yours.

Where is my data stored?

Your sits and Awake log stay on your devices and sync privately across your iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Apple TV through your own iCloud. We don't run a server that collects your practice, and nothing is sold or shared. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Where do the daily reflections come from?

Each day's reflection is a short, universal thought on stillness, presence, and showing up — drawn from a broad collection of contemplative writing. One calm line to carry into your sit; no doctrine, no commentary.

Are there streaks or goals?

No streaks, no targets, no badges. Miss a day or a month and nothing is lost — you simply sit again when you're ready. The Awake log is a quiet record, not a scoreboard.

How much does it cost?

Awakening is free. There are no subscriptions, no ads, and no upsell.

Which devices are supported?

Awakening runs on iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Apple TV, with full feature parity across every platform.
