Awakening — Privacy Policy

Awakening is a quiet place to sit. It is private by design: there are no accounts, no tracking, and nothing leaves your device.

Effective Date: June 27, 2026  ·  Last Updated: June 27, 2026

Short version: Awakening does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data. The app runs entirely on your device. No account, no sign-up, no cloud, no ads, no analytics, no third-party SDKs. What you do in Awakening stays on your phone.

1. Who We Are

Awakening is developed by Mindobix, an independent software studio operated by Ganesh Subramanian, based in Cincinnati, Ohio. This policy applies to the Awakening app on Android (Google Play) and on Apple platforms (iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Apple TV).

2. Information We Collect

None. Awakening does not:

  • Create accounts or require any registration or login
  • Collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any personally identifiable information
  • Collect location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers for advertising
  • Include analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or other third-party SDKs
  • Send your data to any server, cloud service, or third party

3. Where Your Data Lives

Everything you record in Awakening — the days you sat and how long you were awake — is stored locally on your device using on-device storage (SQLite / app preferences). The app functions fully offline and requires no internet connection. Uninstalling Awakening removes all of its data from your device.

On Apple devices, if you have iCloud enabled, your sittings may sync privately between your own Apple devices through your personal iCloud account, governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. Mindobix has no access to that data. The Android version stores data only on the device.

4. Permissions

Awakening requests no sensitive runtime permissions. It does not access the camera, microphone, location, contacts, or files. The optional closing chime is generated on the device and plays through standard audio output.

5. Children's Privacy

Awakening is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because the app collects no data at all, there is nothing for a child (or anyone) to submit.

6. Data Security

Because Awakening does not collect or transmit your data, there is no server-side store to breach. Your information remains on your device, protected by your device's own security.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
