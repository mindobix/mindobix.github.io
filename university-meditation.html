What this is

A student can write directly. So can a faculty member, a student group, or a student-life office. Ganesh Subramanian will sit with a group, or with one student who asked. The setting can be a campus in or near Cincinnati, or a call.

The practice is the one on Two Truths. Keep the semester as it is: classes, rent, friends, sleep. Then protect a short time to ask who is living it. Not by caring less about the degree. By gripping the story of the degree a little less.

What a session is

  • You say who is coming and how many. A class visit, a student group, or one person are all the same practice.
  • No price on this page. If a university is arranging it, the number is in the reply before a date is set. A student writing alone can ask what a single sit costs.
  • The hour is a short talk and a sit. No one is called on to perform being calm.
  • Nothing is counted. A missed week is a missed week.

What it will not do

It will not replace campus counseling, a clinic, or a dean of students. If you are in distress, go there. Ganesh is not a clinician, and this is not a mental-health service.

Not therapy. Not medical advice. If sitting brings up something distressing, stop. The counseling center is the right next door.

Before you write
