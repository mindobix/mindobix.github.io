K-12 Meditation Service

A school or a parent arranges this. The sit is short. The words are ordinary. Ganesh does not sit alone with a child.

Write from a school or home (513) 206-0195

Who arranges it

A principal, a teacher, or a parent writes to Ganesh Subramanian. A child does not book this. A parent or school staff member is in the room for the whole sit. That is not a preference. It is the rule.

The visit can be a classroom, a school hall, or a family at home in or near Cincinnati. A call is possible when a parent is on it with the child. Say which one you want in the first note.

What the children are asked to do

  • Sit quietly for a short time. Younger children get a shorter time. There is no prize for lasting longer.
  • Notice that they can hear the room, and that something in them is already aware of the hearing. Then go back to the day.
  • Hear no story meant to frighten them, and no gold star for being the calmest person in the room.
  • Keep recess, friends, homework, and home exactly as real as they were. This is not an exit from school life.

What a session is

Ganesh speaks in plain words a child can follow, then the room is quiet, then the day resumes. He does not collect names, photos, or a record of who sat still. There is no price on this page. The school or the parent agrees the number in the reply before a date is set.

Not counseling. Not medical advice. If a child is distressed, the sit stops. The school counselor and the family doctor stay the right people for that. This service does not replace them, and it does not ask a child to disclose anything private.

Before you write

If you are the adult arranging this: can you sit in the room for the whole time, and can the children leave the quiet the moment they want to?
