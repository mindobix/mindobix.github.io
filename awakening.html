What it is

Just a still place to sit.

Three quiet things, done well. Nothing more to manage.

A breathing dot

Sit for as long as you like, or set a gentle timer. A soft dot expands and contracts to give your breath something to rest on. Tap BEGIN to start; tap END when you're done.

✶

A daily reflection

One short passage each day, drawn from the world's wisdom traditions. A single thought to carry into the stillness — never a feed, never a notification storm.

A quiet record of the days you showed up — the Awake log — synced across iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Apple TV. No streak to defend, no guilt for missing a day.
