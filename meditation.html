What you are asking for

The free reading lives at twotruths.net. Nothing is sold there. These pages are how you ask Ganesh Subramanian to sit with you.

A session is a short talk in plain words and a time of quiet. Afterward you can say what you actually noticed. There is no attendance score, no level, and no count of the days you came back. If you miss a week, you missed a week.

There is no price list on this site. You write, say which service you want, and whether you want it in Cincinnati, at your place, or on a call. The number is in the reply, before anything starts.

Not therapy. Not medical advice. If sitting brings up something distressing, stop. A counselor or a doctor is the right next person.
